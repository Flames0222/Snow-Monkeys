# Snow Monkeys

A NeoForge mod for **Minecraft 1.21.1** about one animal: the Japanese snow monkey (*Macaca fuscata*),
and the steaming hot springs it soaks in through the winter.

## Features

### Snow Monkey
- Spawns in troops of 3–6 in cold biomes: snowy taiga, snowy plains, grove, snowy slopes, taiga,
  old growth taigas, windswept forest and windswept hills.
- **Soaks in hot springs.** Every so often a monkey walks to a nearby hot spring, sits down in the shallow
  water up to its shoulders, closes its eyes and relaxes, healing as it soaks. It goes back sooner when
  it's snowing.
- **Rolls snowballs.** Adults standing on snow sometimes roll and leave behind a snowball, as real
  macaques do.
- **Snowball fights.** Hit one and the whole troop pelts you with snowballs. They get over it after
  about 15 seconds.
- **Grabs food.** Monkeys eat sweet berries, glow berries, apples, wheat or carrots left on the ground.
  The same foods breed them and make babies grow faster.
- Babies run away when hurt and follow their parents.

### Hot springs
- Generated in cold biomes as uneven pools of milky turquoise **Hot Spring Water**, lined with
  **Onsen Stone**, with a **Hot Spring Vent** in the floor.
- Hot spring water steams, never freezes, and gives anything that soaks in it **Warmth** (stops
  freezing and slowly heals).
- **Build your own onsen:** put a Hot Spring Vent under still water. Over time it heats the water
  above it into hot spring water, and melts nearby snow and ice.
- **Onsen tamago:** drop an egg into a hot spring and wait about 30 seconds. It cooks into an
  **Onsen Egg**, a filling food that also gives Warmth.

### Tectonic compatibility
Hot springs don't use fixed Y levels. They read the generation heightmaps, so they work at any height,
including Tectonic's very tall mountains. On steep ground the feature tries several nearby spots and
smaller pools to find a ledge flat enough. It skips spots under water or on ice, and adds walls on cliff
edges so pools don't spill. Tectonic is listed as an optional dependency and isn't required.

Which biomes get spawns and hot springs is set by biome tags, which a datapack can override:
- `data/snowmonkeys/tags/worldgen/biome/snow_monkey_spawns.json`
- `data/snowmonkeys/tags/worldgen/biome/has_hot_spring.json`

## Building
Requires JDK 21.

```
./gradlew build        # jar is written to build/libs/
./gradlew runClient    # start a dev client
```

Textures are generated by `tools/generate_textures.py` (needs Pillow). Edit it and re-run it to change
the art; the PNG files are committed, so you don't need it to build.

Snow monkey sounds reuse the vanilla fox sounds at a higher pitch through `sounds.json`. A resource
pack can replace them.
